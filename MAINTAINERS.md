## Responsibilities

- Review and approve releases, migrations and security fixes.
- Maintain source, documentation, licence notices and third-party inventory.
- Triage security reports under `SECURITY.md`.
- Keep the privacy, governance and DPG evidence records current.

Do not put personal phone numbers, credentials or private operational contacts in this public file.
