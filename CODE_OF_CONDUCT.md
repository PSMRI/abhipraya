# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and harassment-free community for everyone who contributes to or uses Abhipraya.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive and professional.
- Welcome different experiences and viewpoints.
- Focus discussion on the software, evidence and proposed change.
- Protect confidential information, credentials and personal data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, abuse, doxxing, sharing personal data without permission, or knowingly publishing security vulnerabilities without coordinated disclosure are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately through the contact published in `MAINTAINERS.md` or the project's security contact. Maintainers may warn, moderate, remove content, or restrict participation in response to violations. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
