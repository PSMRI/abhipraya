# Why Abhipraya

## Feedback should lead to better care

People using a public-service location notice service problems first: a long wait, unavailable resources, poor cleanliness, or difficulty finding staff. Yet these experiences are often not recorded in a safe, timely, and usable way. When feedback is informal or fragmented, service teams have limited evidence of where to focus improvement efforts. Healthcare facilities are the current implementation example.

Abhipraya provides a simple, privacy-conscious way to close that gap. A beneficiary can scan a department QR code and share anonymous feedback in a supported language. Authorised teams can then view scoped trends, identify recurring issues, and record corrective and preventive actions (CAPA).

## The need it addresses

- **Accessible public voice:** QR-led surveys make it easy to share feedback at the point of service, without creating an account or providing a name, phone number, or Aadhaar number.
- **Actionable local evidence:** Feedback is associated with the relevant service location and service area so teams can distinguish broad concerns from location-specific gaps.
- **Faster improvement cycles:** Dashboards, reports, and CAPA tracking help turn recurring feedback into documented follow-up rather than isolated complaints.
- **Inclusive communication:** English and Hindi survey support makes participation more practical for a wider range of beneficiaries.
- **Responsible data use:** Anonymous feedback, role-scoped administrative access, and proportionate validation controls support service improvement without using responses to identify or retaliate against people.

## Why an open-source platform

Public-service programmes should be able to understand, review, adapt, and operate the systems used to collect public feedback. Abhipraya is open source so implementing organisations can configure service-location and service-area surveys, examine the privacy and access controls, and adapt the platform to their service context without treating the underlying product as a black box.

The platform is configuration-driven: survey content, languages, facilities, and departments can evolve without rewriting the application or changing the meaning of historical responses. Versioned survey packages preserve the context needed to interpret feedback over time.

## Intended outcome

Abhipraya is not only a feedback collection tool. Its intended outcome is a visible improvement loop:

```text
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ Beneficiary scans a QR code  │
│ and shares anonymous feedback│
└──────────────┬───────────────┘
               │
               ▼
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ Feedback is grouped by       │
│ facility and department      │
└──────────────┬───────────────┘
               │
               ▼
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ Teams review trends and      │
│ identify priority gaps       │
└──────────────┬───────────────┘
               │
               ▼
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ Corrective and preventive    │
│ actions are recorded         │
└──────────────┬───────────────┘
               │
               ▼
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ Improvement is monitored and │
│ informs the next response    │
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

By making the loop easier to run and review, Abhipraya helps healthcare teams listen more consistently, act on evidence, and improve the everyday experience of people using public services.
