# Abhipraya — Every Voice Matters

## Video specification

- **Duration:** 120 seconds
- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps
- **Style:** Warm, Pixar-inspired 3D animation; clean Indian government health-centre setting; realistic but friendly expressions; soft morning light; navy, saffron, white, and healthcare green accents.
- **Important:** Sita Devi, the pharmacist, administrator, doctor, and child must remain visually consistent in every shot.

## Character bible

**Sita Devi:** fictional rural Indian woman, 30–35, simple cotton saree, prescription in hand, calm and hopeful, accompanied by her five-year-old son.

**Pharmacist:** government pharmacist in a white coat, helpful, polite, professional.

**District administrator:** male or female official in formal attire, focused on the dashboard and corrective action.

**Doctor:** smiling government medical officer, caring and approachable.

## Voice-over script

> Every citizen deserves quality healthcare.
>
> Sometimes, challenges like medicine shortages affect service delivery.
>
> With Abhipraya, citizens can anonymously share their experiences by simply scanning a QR code.
>
> No login. No personal information. Just honest feedback.
>
> Health administrators receive real-time insights, identify service gaps, and take corrective action.
>
> Together, anonymous feedback drives better decisions, stronger health systems, and improved care for everyone.
>
> Abhipraya — Every Voice Matters.

## Audio direction

- **0–30s:** Gentle piano, soft birds, light health-centre ambience.
- **30–70s:** Add light orchestral strings as the QR survey and dashboard appear.
- **70–120s:** Uplifting inspirational orchestral theme; resolve warmly on the logo card.
- Keep dialogue and narration clear above music. Avoid dramatic or distressing sound effects.

## Master generation prompt

> Create a polished 3D animated public-health short film for Abhipraya, set in a bright rural Indian government Primary Health Centre. Use consistent characters: Sita Devi, a 30–35-year-old rural Indian woman in a simple cotton saree with her five-year-old son; a polite government pharmacist in a white coat; a focused district administrator; and a caring government doctor. Show an everyday medicine shortage, an Abhipraya QR poster, anonymous multilingual feedback on a smartphone, a district dashboard alert, corrective action, medicine delivery, and improved patient service. Warm sunlight, clean Indian architecture, respectful human expressions, accessible government-service tone, navy/saffron/white/green palette, smooth cinematic camera movement, no brand distortion, no personal data, no login screen. End with the Abhipraya logo and the messages “Anonymous Feedback”, “Better Decisions”, “Better Healthcare”, “Your Identity is Protected”, and “Your Feedback Creates Change”.

## Production safeguards

- Use fictional names and facilities only.
- Keep the QR code decorative/non-scannable in the generated film unless a production QR asset is deliberately composited later.
- Do not show real patient records, phone numbers, faces of identifiable real people, or personal information.
- Add subtitles in English and Hindi during final editing.
