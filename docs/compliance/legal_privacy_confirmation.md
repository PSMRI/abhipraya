## Minimum public notice content

The deployment's public survey must clearly state:

1. why feedback is collected;
2. that submission is anonymous where that is the selected configuration;
3. which optional technical data may be processed (for example, location verification);
4. how long data is retained;
5. who can access aggregated and response-level data; and
6. how a person can contact the deployment owner.
