# Content safeguarding and moderation

## Purpose

Abhipraya is intended for anonymous service feedback. Survey designers must minimise the risk that a respondent submits personal, harmful, unlawful, or abusive content, particularly when a configured survey includes a free-text question.

## Configuration controls

1. Prefer structured questions and response options whenever they meet the programme need.
2. Do not ask for names, contact details, government identifiers, medical records, or allegations about identifiable people.
3. Make free-text questions optional, state that respondents must not include personal or medical information, and set a proportionate maximum length.
4. Version and approve every survey change using the survey publishing process before it is active.

## Operational moderation procedure

The Abhipraya Team operates the following procedure for any approved free-text configuration. Moderation and escalation contact: [abhipraya@piramalswasthya.org](mailto:abhipraya@piramalswasthya.org).

| Stage | Required action | Accountable role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Triage | Review new flagged content within the approved service window. | Abhipraya Team |
| Restrict | Limit access to the minimum authorised reviewers; do not copy content into public reports. | Abhipraya Team |
| Escalate | Follow the organisation's safeguarding, legal, or incident process where content indicates a credible risk. | Abhipraya Team |
| Redact | Remove direct identifiers and sensitive details before operational reporting or evidence sharing. | Abhipraya Team |
| Retain or delete | Apply the approved retention schedule and preserve only the audit information required by policy. | Abhipraya Team |

## Verified deployment evidence

The Bihar deployment report exports reviewed on 29 July 2026 contain **52 rating indicators** and no free-text response field. The deployed survey path therefore does not accept unstructured respondent content that could be inappropriate or illegal. Structured questions, server-side role controls, non-PII export controls and the documented Abhipraya Team escalation route apply to the deployment.

If a future survey enables free-text input, it must use the moderation procedure above, the approved retention schedule, and the public escalation contact. Do not store real complainant details or sensitive incident records in this repository.
