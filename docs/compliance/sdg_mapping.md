# SDG mapping

## Purpose

Abhipraya is an anonymous public-feedback platform. Its healthcare deployment gives beneficiaries a simple way to report their experience and gives authorised officials aggregated evidence to improve services.

## Primary SDG contribution

| SDG | Target area | Abhipraya contribution | Evidence to retain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-being | Access to quality health services | Captures experience at facilities and departments, including medicine availability, waiting time, cleanliness, staff behaviour and other configured indicators. | Configured survey packages, aggregated reports, CAPA records and before/after service indicators. |
| SDG 16 — Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions | Accountable and inclusive institutions | Provides an anonymous channel for people to share service experience and supports transparent, aggregated reporting to officials. | Privacy notice, access-control records, audit logs and aggregated report samples. |
| SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities | Inclusive access to public services | QR-based, multilingual feedback can be configured for local languages and designed for low-literacy, mobile-first use. | Language configuration, accessibility test results and usability feedback. |

## Theory of change

```text
Anonymous feedback
        ↓
Aggregated indicator trends
        ↓
Officials identify low-performing services
        ↓
Corrective and preventive action (CAPA)
        ↓
Follow-up reporting and improved service experience
```

## Measurement approach

The platform itself does not prove health outcomes. It produces operational evidence that an implementing organisation can use to measure improvement:

1. Record the baseline for a configured indicator and time period.
2. Record the corrective action, responsible unit and target date.
3. Compare the same indicator over a later period using the same survey version.
4. Publish only aggregated, non-identifying results.

## Required nomination evidence

Before any Digital Public Goods Alliance submission, publish a short implementation-neutral statement of the intended public benefit, the SDG links above, and evidence from one or more deployments. Do not claim causal impact unless it has been independently evaluated.
